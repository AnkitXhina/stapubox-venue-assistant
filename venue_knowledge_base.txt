--- Tennis Court Rules ---
StapuBox Arena Central has 8 outdoor hard courts and 4 indoor clay courts. Rules are as follows:
1. All players must wear proper tennis shoes with non-marking soles. Street shoes, sandals, and running shoes are prohibited on court surfaces.
2. Court sessions are limited to 60 minutes during peak hours (6:00 AM – 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM – 8:00 PM) and 90 minutes during off-peak hours.
3. Advance booking is required. Walk-in play is available only if courts are unoccupied.
4. Players must arrive within 10 minutes of their booking time or the reservation will be forfeited.
5. Ball machines are available for rental on courts 1–4 (outdoor) only. Ball machine use is prohibited on indoor clay courts.
6. Coaching sessions must be conducted by StapuBox-certified instructors. Outside coaches require prior written approval from the venue manager.
7. Food and glass containers are not allowed on the courts. Only water bottles with sealed caps are permitted.
8. Players under 16 must be accompanied by an adult or a registered coach at all times.

--- Basketball Court Rules ---
StapuBox Arena Central has 3 indoor regulation basketball courts with professional-grade maple flooring. Rules are as follows:
1. Non-marking indoor basketball shoes are mandatory. Players wearing outdoor shoes will not be permitted on the court.
2. Full-court games are limited to 5-on-5. Half-court games allow up to 4-on-4.
3. Dunking is allowed on courts 1 and 2 only. Court 3 has adjustable-height rims for junior training and dunking is prohibited.
4. Open gym sessions run from 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM on weekdays and 9:00 AM – 11:00 AM on weekends (first come, first served).
5. Team bookings for league play require a minimum 48-hour advance reservation and a refundable damage deposit of $50.
6. Personal music speakers are not allowed. The facility provides a sound system with pre-set playlists during open gym sessions.
7. All players must sign a liability waiver before first use. Waivers are valid for 12 months.

--- Swimming Pool Rules ---
The swimming pool at StapuBox Arena Central is an Olympic-size (50-meter), 10-lane pool with a separate children's wading area. Water temperature is maintained at 27°C (80°F). Rules include:
1. All swimmers must shower before entering the pool. Shower facilities are located adjacent to the pool entrance.
2. Proper swimwear is required: competitive swimsuits, swim trunks, or rash guards. Cotton clothing, cut-off shorts, and street clothes are strictly prohibited.
3. Swim caps are mandatory for all swimmers with hair longer than shoulder length.
4. Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult (18+) at all times. The adult must be in the water if the child is in the deep end (depth greater than 1.5 meters).
5. Lane swimming etiquette: slow lanes (lanes 1–3), medium lanes (4–7), fast lanes (8–10). Swimmers must follow the counter-clockwise circle swimming pattern.
6. Diving is permitted only from the designated diving blocks during supervised lap sessions. No diving in the wading area.
7. Inflatables, kickboards, and training fins are allowed in designated lanes only. No inflatable toys in lap lanes.
8. The pool is closed for 15 minutes every 2 hours for water quality checks (at 8 AM, 10 AM, 12 PM, 2 PM, 4 PM, 6 PM, and 8 PM).
9. Lifeguards are on duty during all operating hours. Maximum pool capacity is 120 persons.

--- Cricket Net Rules ---
Greenfield Sports Complex has 12 professional-grade cricket practice nets with synthetic turf pitches and 4 nets with natural turf wickets. Rules include:
1. Proper cricket gear is mandatory: batting pads, gloves, helmet (for batters facing fast bowling), and abdominal guard. The complex is not liable for injuries resulting from inadequate protective gear.
2. Each net session is limited to 45 minutes during peak hours (6:00 AM – 8:00 AM and 4:00 PM – 7:00 PM) and 60 minutes during off-peak.
3. Bowling machines are available in nets 1–6. Speed settings must not exceed 140 km/h. Bowling machine use requires a signed safety acknowledgment.
4. Natural turf nets (nets 9–12) are reserved for advanced players and registered club teams. A proficiency assessment may be required for first-time users.
5. No metal spikes on synthetic pitches. Rubber-soled cricket shoes only on synthetic surfaces. Metal spikes are permitted on natural turf nets only.
6. Hard cricket balls (156g regulation) are used in nets 1–8. Only approved soft training balls (Incrediball or equivalent) are allowed in nets for juniors (under 14).
7. Bowlers must not bowl from closer than the designated bowling crease mark. Short-pitched bowling is limited to 2 bouncers per over in practice sessions.
8. All participants must complete a cricket-specific safety induction before first use.

--- Football Pitch Rules ---
Greenfield Sports Complex has 4 full-size FIFA-standard football pitches (natural grass) and 2 all-weather 3G artificial pitches. Rules are:
1. Footwear: Moulded studs or turf shoes on natural grass. Only rubber-moulded studs or astro-turf shoes on 3G pitches. Metal studs are strictly prohibited on artificial surfaces.
2. Shin guards are mandatory for all match play and strongly recommended for training sessions.
3. Full-size pitch bookings are for 90-minute slots. The pitch must be vacated within 10 minutes of the booking end time.
4. Teams must provide their own corner flags and goals nets are pre-installed. Match balls are not provided; teams must bring their own.
5. Natural grass pitches may be closed during or after heavy rainfall to protect the surface. In such cases, teams will be offered 3G pitch alternatives or full refunds.
6. Maximum of 22 players per full-size pitch. 7-a-side pitches (marked on pitches 3 and 4) allow a maximum of 16 players.
7. No glass bottles, smoking, or alcohol on or near the pitches at any time.
8. Floodlights are included in the booking fee for evening slots. Floodlight operation begins at dusk and is managed by facility staff.
9. Dog walking, cycling, and unauthorized drone use over the pitches is prohibited.

--- Badminton Hall Rules ---
Greenfield Sports Complex has 8 indoor badminton courts with BWF-approved synthetic flooring and professional lighting. Rules include:
1. Non-marking badminton or indoor court shoes are mandatory. The facility reserves the right to inspect footwear before court entry.
2. Court sessions are 60 minutes during peak hours and 90 minutes during off-peak.
3. Shuttlecocks: feather shuttlecocks are required on courts 1–4 (competition courts). Nylon shuttlecocks are allowed on courts 5–8 (recreational courts).
4. Maximum of 4 players per court (doubles play). Singles play is allowed but does not extend the booking duration.
5. Coaching is available through the facility's in-house coaching team. External coaches are not permitted without prior written authorization.
6. The hall temperature is maintained at 20–22°C. Doors must remain closed during play to avoid air current interference.
7. Spectators are welcome in the designated viewing gallery. No food or drinks near the courts.
8. Junior sessions (under 16) run from 3:00 PM – 5:00 PM on weekdays with dedicated coaching staff.

--- Squash Court Rules ---
Summit Athletic Club has 6 international-standard glass-backed squash courts, including 2 championship show courts with spectator seating for 150. Rules include:
1. Proper squash shoes with non-marking, gum-rubber soles are mandatory. The club strictly enforces this policy to protect the specialist maple court floors.
2. Protective eyewear is mandatory for all players. The club sells approved eyewear at the pro shop. Players without eyewear will not be permitted to play.
3. Court sessions are 45 minutes during peak hours (6:00 AM – 8:30 AM and 5:00 PM – 8:00 PM) and 60 minutes during off-peak.
4. The show courts (courts 5 and 6) are available for general booking but may be reserved for tournaments and exhibitions with 7 days' notice.
5. Solo practice sessions (ghosting/drilling) are allowed during off-peak hours only and are limited to 30 minutes if other players are waiting.
6. The court temperature is maintained at 18–20°C for optimal playing conditions. Do not tamper with climate controls.
7. Players must wipe down the court walls and floor if excessive sweat or condensation is present. Squeegees are provided in each court.
8. Only official WSF-approved squash balls are permitted. The pro shop stocks balls of all speed ratings (double yellow dot, single yellow, red, blue).
9. Juniors under 14 must play with a coach or an adult member present on court.

--- Gym & Fitness Center Rules ---
The Summit Athletic Club gym spans 15,000 sq ft and includes cardio machines, free weights, resistance machines, a functional training zone, and a dedicated stretching area. Rules include:
1. Proper athletic footwear is required at all times. Open-toed shoes, sandals, and boots are not permitted in the gym.
2. Towels must be placed on all equipment during use. Complimentary towels are available at the gym entrance.
3. All equipment must be wiped down with the provided sanitizing solution after each use.
4. Re-rack all weights after use. Failure to re-rack weights will result in a verbal warning; repeat offenses may lead to a 7-day gym suspension.
5. Maximum equipment use time during peak hours: 20 minutes per cardio machine, 15 minutes per weight station. Rotate if others are waiting.
6. Personal trainers: only Summit-certified personal trainers may conduct sessions in the gym. External trainers must apply for guest trainer accreditation ($100/month).
7. No chalk use except in the designated deadlift/Olympic lifting platform area.
8. Dropping weights is prohibited except on the Olympic lifting platforms. Controlled lowering is required on all other equipment.
9. Photography and video recording require prior approval from the front desk to respect other members' privacy.
10. The gym offers group fitness classes (yoga, spinning, HIIT, Pilates) in the dedicated studio. Class schedules are posted weekly and booking is required through the app.

--- Indoor Volleyball Court Rules ---
Summit Athletic Club has 3 indoor volleyball courts with FIVB-standard dimensions and professional sprung flooring. Rules include:
1. Non-marking indoor court shoes are mandatory. Ankle braces are recommended but not required.
2. Standard team size is 6-on-6 for full games. Recreational 4-on-4 is allowed on courts 2 and 3 only.
3. Court bookings are in 60-minute blocks. Teams of 12+ players may book 90-minute extended sessions.
4. Net height is set to 2.43m (men's) or 2.24m (women's) per FIVB standards. Requests for custom net heights for recreational play must be made at booking time.
5. Referee services are available for competitive matches at $30/match (must be booked 48 hours in advance).
6. Open play sessions are held Tuesday and Thursday evenings (7:00 PM – 9:00 PM) and Saturday mornings (9:00 AM – 11:00 AM). Drop-in fee applies.
7. Knee pads are strongly recommended. The club provides loaner knee pads at the front desk (limited supply).
8. Volleyballs are provided for all court bookings. Teams may bring their own FIVB-approved balls for match play.
9. Water bottles only near the court. No food, glass, or energy drink cans on the court surface.
